# Blended: case study notes

Running log for the portfolio case study. Add to it as decisions get made.

## The idea
Blended is a sustainable clothing shop built around a **capsule wardrobe**. Every piece is curated to go with every other piece, so any top works with any pair of pants.

## Core features
- **Change room** (the shopping bag). Two carousels: tops on top, pants below. Swipe each one to try different combinations of the pieces you've added, before you buy.
- **Closet.** Shows every combination your pieces make.
- **Turn around.** Flips every piece, added or not, between front and back view.
- **Left/right-handed mode** (coded app only). Mirrors the side buttons on the change room screen so they sit under your thumb. Cut from the Figma prototype, which has one right-hand layout.

## How the project evolved
1. **Sustainable fashion shop** (Desktop UI page). A full e-commerce site with Women/Men, a blog, a sustainability page, designer accounts, and a capsule wardrobe feed.
2. **Capsule wardrobe setup** (older Mobile UI sections). Onboarding asks for body measurements, fit preference (super tight to super loose) and sizes, then shows a "My Wardrobe" screen with arrow carousels and a "Total Fits" grid.
3. **Events + Change Room** (the "this should be the final result" section). The flow narrows to two tabs: shop by occasion, then mix and match in the change room.
4. **Shopify add-on idea** (2026-10-20). The change room is the part with the most value, so it could plug into any store, with merchants curating their own capsules.

Research to pull into the case study later: the Midterm and Final presentation pages, Surveys, Personas/Stories/Flows/IA, Competitors, User testing, Paper wireframes.

## Open problems

- **Bag vs cart: the change room or a cart?** The change room already holds everything you'll buy, but the buy button says "ADD TO CART" and the app opens "Your cart", so you see two containers and can't tell whether your pieces are already in the cart. Should the buy button be "CHECKOUT", "BUY ALL" or "BUY THIS OUTFIT", with the change room as the only container? (Solved 2026-11-01: "Checkout" plus the total, built in the prototype; coded app not confirmed.)
- **Two sets of event names.** Figma has 8 events ("Office hours", "Day off", "Working out", "Night out", "Beach day"…) and the coded app has 7 ("Work", "Beach", "Evening", "Business casual"…), including two near-duplicates. The Figma notification already advertises "Night out", which doesn't exist in the app. Does the app take Figma's 8 names, does Figma take the app's 7, or does blend get a new shared set? (Decided 2026-11-01: the coded app takes Figma's 8 names; being applied.)
- **Settings vs Options.** Figma, the glossary and the menu say "Options" with "Controls on: Left / Right"; the coded app's sheet title and menu name say "Settings" with "Dominant hand".
