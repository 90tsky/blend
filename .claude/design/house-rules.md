# blend house rules

The rules every helper follows when it designs, writes, animates or reviews in the Blend-remake Figma file. design-reviewer checks them; ux-ui-designer, ux-writer and figma-animator follow them. Moved here from design-reviewer on 2026-11-01. When CASE-STUDY-NOTES.md has a later dated decision, the notes win; update this file to match.


**Layout and spacing**
- Visible content sits at least 12 (layout/margin) from the left and right edges. Content columns are 406 wide at x=12 on the 430 frame. Judge the visible, clipped bounds only.
- Margin exceptions, not issues: full-width bars with their own fill (top bar 430x70, bottom bar 430x128, Options sticky header 730:3699); full-width wrappers with no fill; event banners and event-page heroes (full-bleed by design); the screen's own fill; invisible 48x48 hit areas around small icons; content clipped inside a card; hidden or opacity-0 layers parked off-screen; change-room carousel peeks (garment · prev / next). The rule is sides only. Top and bottom are set by the bars.
- The side padding of screen content and the top bar's page nav is bound to layout/margin. The Options sticky header has 0 sides and bottom since 2026-11-02, because the header inside it carries the 12 margins.
- Bottom bar: a tabs row, then an actions row, each 48 tall. The actions row ends 12 above the screen bottom.
- At the end of the scroll, the last content ends at least 12 above the bottom bar, so the bar never covers it.
- Spacing uses only the tokens scale (space/*, layout/*, card/padding). Off-grid values such as 5, 10, 14 or 22 are findings.
- Offsets that clear fixed bars may stay typed (sticky header paddingTop 70, Options content paddingTop 183, a 24 gap, top-bar page nav 11/11), but the visible gap they leave should land on the scale.
- Options: three groups (profile, preferences, account). 48 between groups (layout/group), 24 between settings (layout/section), 8 from label to control (space/8). Hints sit under the control. No divider lines.

**Tap targets**
- Every tap target is at least 48x48. Small icons (+, back, close) keep their look inside an invisible 48x48 tap area.
- Buttons, fields and pickers are 48 tall, bound to control/height (or size/control). Icons are 24 (size/icon).
- Icons come from one set: Material Symbols Outlined. Each icon is one vector drawn by its FILL only (no stroke), with a live area of about 18 inside the 24 frame (camera 18x16, menu 18x12), coloured color/on-surface. A custom icon (e.g. camera + sparkle) must be built the same way: outline the strokes, flatten to one fill-only vector, no frame stroke or stray outline, and match the set's line weight. (Added 2026-11-01 after the first camera + sparkle came out stroked, oversized and boxed.)
- Icon line ends are round: bars and open lines end in a half-circle of their own weight (menu, search handle, Turn around arrow and line, ✕, back, +, ticks). The point of the Turn around arrowhead stays sharp. Outline shapes (e.g. the ai_sparkle stars) are empty, with the set's line weight. If a small shape's inside would close up at 24, enlarge the shape rather than thinning the line. (Added 2026-11-01, user asked for round ends and empty stars.)
- The notes say a hotspot with no fill ignores taps and needs a 1% fill. Many tap layers in the file have no fill, yet adding from cards worked in Chrome. Report all such hits as ONE row with severity "Question · needs a play-test" (not Must-fix), with the count and one example, and suggest the test: tap beside a number on a size picker, or the corner of a card's + area. Raise it to Must-fix only if the change added a new no-fill tap layer with no painted child under most of it.
- One reaction per trigger. A reaction added on a nested instance layer adds to the component's own, and both fire.
- The toast's top padding of 32 in its hidden, in and out states is its slide-in offset: leave it typed. Since 2026-11-01 the shown states line up from the bottom (top padding 0), and a 10-tall "bottom offset" layer with no fill keeps the pill's bottom 12 above whatever sits under it, so two-line toasts grow upwards. Measure that 12 from the top edge of the bottom bar's background (not the nav switch inside it) on Events and event pages, and from the top of the button under it on Product, and from the top of the "Use a model instead" link on Try-On · photo, where the toast may cover the tips while it shows (decided 2026-11-01). Never link the 32, the 0 or the 10 to tokens, never give "bottom offset" a fill, and don't report them as unbound numbers. (Linking the padding earlier on 2026-11-01 pinned the toast 20px low, over the nav switch.)
- Hit areas (tap, shield, remove shield, undo hotspot, empty top/bottom/all fills) stay plain white at 1% on purpose, with no colour variable. The prototype player draws a variable-linked fill at full strength and ignores the paint's opacity (found 2026-11-01: they showed as white blocks). Never report these as unbound colours. The Aplus2 notch covers (notch cover · right/bottom in the empty boxes) carry the same plain 1% white on top of their linked background fill on purpose, so they match the box's tap fill.

**Tokens**
- Padding, gaps (grid row and column gaps too), radius and control sizes are bound to the tokens collection (VariableCollectionId:857:123), never typed.
- Use the right kind of token: space/*, layout/* and card/padding for gaps; radius/* for corners; size/* and control/height for width and height. A radius bound to space/12 is wrong.
- Never bind to the legacy 'all' variables padding, rounding-product-card, event-card-lower-margin, black or white. The 'all' collection is for prototype state only.

**Colour**
- Fills, strokes and gradient stops are bound to the theme collection (VariableCollectionId:549:9348, light and dark). No raw hex, no library colours.
- Secondary text (subtitles, hints, notes, status, address and time lines) uses color/hint. Links, size-guide numbers and captions on photos keep their own style.
- Unselected labels in switches and pickers use color/on-surface at full strength. The raised knob shows the selection.
- Every button and knob shares color/surface and the same neutral shadow (effect/glow, effect/contact, effect/top light, effect/bottom shade). No gold "selected" glow, no black or grey glow, Checkout included.
- The + add circle on cards (add-toggle, 24 on size/icon since 2026-11-01, color/primary) is a small icon, not a button: its dark fill is intended.
- Intended in dark mode, not bugs: banner titles stay white (unbound #ffffff; at most suggest a fixed token), product photos sit on color/photo plate, and the gender photo card stays white with only a drop shadow.
- You can't see dark mode. Review the bindings, and ask the main chat to flip Appearance in the prototype for a visual check.

**Prototype logic (mark every finding here "needs a play-test")**
- Conditionals are if/else only. A third block silently becomes the else; nested conditionals are rejected.
- After delay only starts when a layer arrives by load, navigation or Change to, never when a variable shows it or switches its variant.
- Copying a screen by script silently drops its links to other screens: after copying, re-copy every link from the original and check each frame (found 2026-11-02). Copying a flow's starting screen (e.g. Events) also copies its flow starting point: only Events 320:7350 ("blend · main") and Welcome 578:9421 ("blend · from sign in") start flows, so remove any other (4 duplicates were removed on 2026-11-02).
- Editing links: change the main component's link first, then any instance that overrides it; editing an override first can make Figma stack duplicate taps on that layer (seen 2026-11-02). After bulk link edits, scan for layers with two identical taps.
- Transitions (2026-11-02, the user's choices): open detail (event pages, Product, Search) = slide in from the right 300ms ease-in-out with match layers, Back mirrors; Try-On is a sheet: rises 300ms ease-out, ✕ slides down 250ms ease-in; steps inside Try-On fade 250ms; error/empty states by number key fade 200ms; leaving a flow for the shop fades 250ms; pop-ups behave like toasts (rise 20px + fade 250ms, ~2.5s, leave 200ms).
- A Change to on a variable-bound instance only sticks if the same action list sets the variable to match. Only stage-level layers Change the stage. In other variable-bound components, taps only set the variable. Exception since 2026-11-01: the gender picker's taps first Change to the target variant (Smart animate, Gentle 0.383s, for the brown rotating background) and then set profile/gender in the same action list, which follows the rule above (needs a play-test: a direct switch once stopped responding on 2026-10-31). Its saturation layers only show grey. The hover/press hint is the "photo hint · gender" layer (1648:208) on each photo, shown by the prototype variables logic/gender hint women / men: the photo's frame sets its hint on Mouse enter and Mouse down and clears it on Mouse leave, and every tap clears both. The hint has no reactions of its own and appears instantly (variables don't animate). Its tap areas keep a plain 1% white fill on purpose (rebuilt 2026-11-02 after a stuck hint in Chrome).
- Nested instances keep their own state in each variant, even with the same name: a hover or press state left on when a tap switches the variant comes back the next time that variant shows. Keep hover state in variables and clear it in the tap (2026-11-02).
- A button's own press feedback (the "face" layer's Mouse down → Change to the pressed state) swallows a tap set on the instance around it: in Chrome the camera's timer button never fired until the face's reaction was removed (2026-11-02). - A variant property bound to a variable picks the FIRST variant that matches the variable's value when the variable changes before you arrive. The bottom bar has two tab=events versions (filter off / on), so the change room's + (which sets nav/tab = events) landed on the filter-off bar. Keep "tab" unbound (static "events") on every filter=on bottom bar, and don't add a second variant that shares a bound property's value without checking this (2026-11-02).
- Animating a variable-bound toggle (2026-11-02, the user: the card + and Checkout "need it to be smart animation on all interactions"). Variables never animate, and a Change to on a variable-bound instance also writes that variable (two-way). So a Change to placed before the logic flips the state twice ("Removed" instead of "Added"), and one placed after a Set variable can't animate (the binding has already swapped it). The pattern that works is add-toggle 325:7407's "phase" twins:
  - settled = normal;
  - the tap first does an instant Change to the twin that looks the same (from plus / from check);
  - the variable flip then lands on a variant that still shows the old look, and 0.01s later it morphs (Smart animate 0.2s ease-out) to settled.
  - A flip made by another layer (Undo on the toast) doesn't start a twin's timer, so a morph must end on settled; otherwise the card gets stuck on the wrong look. Undo stays instant but correct.
  - All 437 card + taps on Screens start with this step. New cards copied from them keep it; a card built by hand needs it added.
